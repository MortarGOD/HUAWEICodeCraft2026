# Standard-HiF4 random benchmark (v18-v28)

Date: 2026-08-22

## Evaluator and dataset audit

`tools/official_benchmark.py` implements the 0819-V2 scoring formula directly:

`100 * (MSE_STD - MSE_PLAYER) / MSE_STD`

`MSE_STD` is generated by the organizer-supplied `hif4_quant.py`, not by a
submitted solution proxy. Candidate calibration and dynamic quantization time
is measured separately from dequantization, MatMul/Attention, and scoring.

The supplied `rand_sample` contains 50 Linear and 50 Attention groups, each
with five calibration and five independent test samples. There are no exact
calibration/test twins. All 50 full Linear signatures and all 50 full Attention
signatures are distinct.

The generator also produced cases for which the published score is undefined:
standard MSE is exactly zero in Linear groups 6, 7, 25, 26, 34, 44, 45 and
Attention groups 5, 22, 39. These groups are audited and reported, then
excluded from comparative totals rather than assigned an artificial score.

Whole-intersection correlation is Pearson -0.432 and Spearman -0.830. Local
quantization time versus online elapsed time has Pearson 0.040. Therefore the
new dataset is a strong robustness/failure detector, but its all-shape equal
weight total is not an online score estimator. In particular, a few synthetic
layouts contribute unbounded negative scores after v22 and reverse the online
trend.

Shape-family aggregation is more informative. Across all ten versions, Linear
families `(2048,4096)`, `(8192,2048)`, and `(4096,1024)` have Pearson
correlations 0.991, 0.991, and 0.985, although much of this signal is the v18
collapse. On v19-v27, Attention families `(16,16,256,mha)`,
`(16,2,256,gqa)`, and `(32,1,128,mqa)` have Pearson correlations 0.904, 0.871,
and 0.857. These panels may be used as gates, but not fitted as a weighted
online oracle with only ten feedback points.

## v28 decision

The replay exposed two related v27 Attention defects. Sparse calibration
layouts still received a Hadamard-64 transform, and `head_dim=32` could then
attempt a reshape with zero blocks. v28 uses a full-head reciprocal Q/K
transform only when the existing calibration density test says the layout is
dense; sparse layouts retain reciprocal balance with an identity transform.

On all valid random Attention groups, v28 completes 47/47 while v27 fails
three. On the 44 common successful groups, v28 improves the official score
from -20155.855 to 5182.602: 24 wins, 12 ties, and 8 losses. The full v28
Attention score is 5412.720. Candidate quantization time is 34.67 seconds
versus 32.80 for v27 in the same run. The full random-data format/state check
passes 1100/1100.

The attempted Linear drift fallback is rejected from v28. It reduced extreme
losses but remained negative and would mix two causal changes into one online
probe. Linear is byte-identical to v27.

Online estimate is deliberately conservative: 17250-17600 points and
185-205 seconds. The random aggregate magnitude is not transported to the
online scale; v28 is primarily a correctness and tail-risk improvement.
